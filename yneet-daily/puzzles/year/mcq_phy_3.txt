# Mixed physics, extra questions
The SI unit of thermal conductivity is: | W m^-1 K^-1 | W m K | J K | W/K | Heat flow rate per unit area per unit temperature gradient.

Wavelength of a 500 Hz sound wave moving at 340 m/s is: | 0.68 m | 1.47 m | 170 m | 840 m | lambda = v/f.
The focal length of a lens of power -2 D is: | -50 cm | 50 cm | -2 m | 2 cm | f = 1/P metres.
A concave lens always forms an image that is: | Virtual, erect and diminished | Real and inverted | Magnified | Real and erect | Used to correct myopia.
The energy of a photon of wavelength 1240 nm is about: | 1 eV | 12.4 eV | 0.1 eV | 124 eV | E (eV) = 1240 / lambda (nm).
Half-life of a radioactive sample is 2 days. Fraction left after 6 days is: | 1/8 | 1/3 | 1/6 | 1/4 | Three half-lives pass.
Alpha decay reduces mass number by: | 4 | 2 | 1 | 0 | It also reduces atomic number by 2.
Beta-minus decay increases atomic number by: | 1 | 2 | 0 | 4 | A neutron becomes a proton.
Gamma decay changes: | Neither mass number nor atomic number | Mass number by 4 | Atomic number by 1 | Atomic number by 2 | Only energy is released.

Specific heat of a substance is the heat needed to raise the temperature of: | 1 kg by 1 K | 1 mole by 1 K | 1 g by 100 K | Any mass by 1 K | Its SI unit is J/kg K.
