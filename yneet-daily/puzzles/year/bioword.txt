CALCIUM | Element in the middle lamella | Calcium is needed for spindle formation.
SULPHUR | Element in cysteine and methionine | Sulphur is also part of coenzyme A.
RUBISCO | Enzyme that fixes CO2 in the Calvin cycle | RuBisCO also has oxygenase activity.
PIGMENT | Coloured molecule that absorbs light | Chlorophyll a is the main pigment of photosynthesis.
QUANTUM | Discrete packet of light energy | One photon carries one quantum of energy.
CITRATE | First product of the Krebs cycle | Citrate forms from acetyl CoA and oxaloacetate.
ETHANOL | Product of yeast fermentation | Yeast tolerates only up to about 13 percent ethanol.
LACTATE | Product of anaerobic respiration in muscle | Lactic acid builds up during intense exercise.
OXIDASE | Enzyme that removes electrons | Cytochrome oxidase is complex IV of the ETS.
MIMICRY | Resemblance used for protection | The Monarch butterfly is avoided by predators because it tastes bad.
TROPHIC | Relating to feeding levels | Producers are at the first trophic level.
BIOMASS | Mass of living matter | Biomass is usually measured as dry weight.
PIONEER | First species to colonise bare land | Lichens are pioneers on bare rock.
HOTSPOT | Biodiversity-rich, threatened region | India has the Western Ghats, Himalaya and Indo-Burma hotspots.
ENDEMIC | Found only in one place | Hotspots have high levels of endemic species.
EXTINCT | Species no longer alive | The Steller's sea cow is extinct.

FOLLICLE | Ovary structure containing an oocyte | The Graafian follicle releases the ovum.
TRICHOME | Epidermal hair on the shoot | Multicellular trichomes reduce water loss.
LENTICEL | Pore in bark for gas exchange | Lenticels are found in woody trees.
PERIDERM | Phellem, phellogen and phelloderm | Periderm replaces the epidermis in old stems.
MERISTEM | Tissue of actively dividing cells | Apical meristems cause growth in length.
TELOMERE | Protective end of a chromosome | Telomeres shorten with each cell division.
FIMBRIAE | Finger-like ends of the fallopian tube | Fimbriae help collect the ovum.
MESOSOME | Infolding of the bacterial cell membrane | Mesosomes help in DNA replication and respiration.
TRACHEID | Elongated xylem cell with tapering ends | Tracheids are the main water conductors in gymnosperms.
SCLEREID | Short, stony sclerenchyma cell | Sclereids give pears their gritty texture.
PERIANTH | Calyx and corolla look alike | The perianth of lily is made of tepals.
ALEURONE | Protein-rich layer of a maize grain | The aleurone layer surrounds the endosperm.
PERICARP | Wall of a fruit | The pericarp has epicarp, mesocarp and endocarp.
EPICARP | Outer skin of a fruit | In mango, the epicarp is the thin skin.
MESOCARP | Fleshy middle wall of a fruit | We eat the mesocarp of mango.
NATALITY | Birth rate in a population | Natality adds individuals to a population.
PARASITE | Organism that lives at the expense of its host | Cuscuta is a parasitic plant.
PREDATOR | Animal that hunts other animals | Predators help keep prey populations in check.
PRODUCER | Organism that makes its own food | Green plants are producers.
ANTIBODY | Y-shaped defence protein | Each antibody has two heavy and two light chains.
MONOCYTE | Largest white blood cell | Monocytes become macrophages in tissues.
BASOPHIL | WBC that releases histamine | Basophils are the rarest WBCs.
DIATOM | Protist with silica shells | Diatoms are called the chief producers in the oceans.
