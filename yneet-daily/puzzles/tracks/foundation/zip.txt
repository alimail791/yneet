# Pathway Zip for Class 9–10. Format: Title | step 1 > step 2 > ... (4 to 7 steps, in the correct order)
Levels of organisation | Cell > Tissue > Organ > Organ system > Organism
Classification ranks | Kingdom > Phylum > Class > Order > Family > Genus > Species
Path of air | Nostrils > Pharynx > Larynx > Trachea > Bronchi > Bronchioles > Alveoli
Digestion | Mouth > Oesophagus > Stomach > Small intestine > Large intestine
Making urine | Renal artery > Glomerulus > Bowman's capsule > Tubule > Collecting duct > Ureter > Bladder
Blood through the heart | Vena cava > Right atrium > Right ventricle > Lungs > Left atrium > Left ventricle > Aorta
Reflex arc | Receptor > Sensory neuron > Spinal cord > Motor neuron > Effector muscle
Nerve impulse | Dendrite > Cell body > Axon > Nerve ending > Synapse
Light into the eye | Cornea > Pupil > Lens > Retina > Optic nerve > Brain
Sound into the ear | Pinna > Ear canal > Eardrum > Ear bones > Cochlea > Auditory nerve
Pollen to seed | Pollen lands on stigma > Pollen tube grows > Reaches ovule > Fertilisation > Seed
Human development | Fertilisation > Zygote > Embryo > Foetus > Baby
Energy flow | Sun > Producers > Herbivores > Carnivores > Top carnivores
Aerobic respiration | Glucose > Pyruvate > In mitochondria > Carbon dioxide and water > Energy released
Water cycle | Evaporation > Condensation > Clouds > Rain > Rivers and oceans
Changes of state on heating | Solid > Melting > Liquid > Boiling > Gas
Models of the atom | Dalton's model > Thomson's plum pudding > Rutherford's nuclear model > Bohr's orbits
Electron shells | K shell > L shell > M shell > N shell
Mole calculation | Mass of substance > Divide by molar mass > Number of moles > Multiply by Avogadro number > Number of particles
Reactivity series (top to bottom) | Potassium > Sodium > Calcium > Magnesium > Aluminium > Zinc > Iron
Extraction of iron | Iron ore > Roasting > Reduction with carbon > Molten iron > Steel making
Making soap | Oil or fat > Add sodium hydroxide > Heat > Saponification > Soap
Alkanes in order | Methane > Ethane > Propane > Butane > Pentane
pH from acidic to basic | Gastric juice > Lemon juice > Pure water > Baking soda solution > Sodium hydroxide
Using SONAR | Ship sends ultrasound > Waves travel down > Reflect from seabed > Echo received > Depth calculated
Rocket launch (third law) | Fuel burns > Hot gases pushed down > Gases push the rocket up > Rocket rises
Energy in a pendulum | Highest point: potential energy > Swinging down > Lowest point: kinetic energy > Swinging up
Electricity at home | Power station > Transmission lines > Electric meter > Fuse > Wiring to appliances
Electric motor | Current flows in coil > Force on coil in field > Coil rotates > Split ring reverses current > Keeps rotating
Rainbow formation | Sunlight enters raindrop > Refraction > Dispersion > Internal reflection > Rainbow seen
Colours of the spectrum | Violet > Indigo > Blue > Green > Yellow > Orange > Red
Hydroelectric power | Water stored in dam > Falls through pipes > Turns turbine > Generator > Electricity
