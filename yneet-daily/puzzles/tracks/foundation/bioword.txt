# Science Word answers for Class 9–10. Format: WORD | hint | fact. 4 to 7 letters, letters only.
TISSUE | Group of cells with a similar job | Xylem and phloem are plant tissues; blood is an animal tissue.
OSMOSIS | Movement of water across a membrane | Water moves from a dilute to a concentrated solution.
VACUOLE | Large storage sac in plant cells | The vacuole keeps the plant cell firm (turgid).
PLASTID | Organelle such as the chloroplast | Plastids are found only in plant cells.
NEURON | Nerve cell | The neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
SYNAPSE | Gap between two neurons | Chemical messengers carry the signal across the synapse.
INSULIN | Hormone that lowers blood sugar | Insulin is made by the pancreas; its lack causes diabetes.
NEPHRON | Filtering unit of the kidney | Each kidney has about a million nephrons.
ENZYME | Biological catalyst | Pepsin digests proteins in the stomach.
ALVEOLI | Tiny air sacs in the lungs | Gas exchange happens through the thin walls of the alveoli.
PHLOEM | Tissue that carries food in plants | Food moves from leaves to other parts by translocation.
GAMETE | Reproductive cell | Gametes have half the number of chromosomes.
ALLELE | One form of a gene | Mendel's tall and dwarf traits are controlled by alleles.
FOSSIL | Preserved remains of an ancient organism | Archaeopteryx links reptiles and birds.
OZONE | Gas that absorbs harmful UV rays | CFCs damage the ozone layer.
VACCINE | Gives immunity without causing the disease | Vaccines train the immune system to fight germs.
ATOM | Smallest particle of an element | Dalton proposed his atomic theory in 1808.
PROTON | Positively charged particle in the nucleus | The number of protons is the atomic number.
NEUTRON | Neutral particle in the nucleus | Neutrons were discovered by James Chadwick in 1932.
ISOTOPE | Atoms of one element with different masses | Carbon-12 and carbon-14 are isotopes.
VALENCY | Combining capacity of an atom | Carbon has a valency of 4.
ALKALI | Base that dissolves in water | Sodium hydroxide is an alkali.
ESTER | Sweet-smelling compound from acid and alcohol | Esters are used in perfumes and flavours.
ALLOY | Mixture of a metal with other elements | Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc.
OXIDE | Compound of an element with oxygen | Metal oxides are usually basic.
WEIGHT | Force with which Earth pulls an object | Weight is measured in newtons and is less on the Moon.
INERTIA | Resistance to change in motion | Heavier objects have more inertia.
JOULE | SI unit of work and energy | One joule is the work done by 1 N over 1 m.
WATT | SI unit of power | One watt is one joule per second.
PRISM | Glass block that splits white light | Newton used a prism to show the spectrum.
RETINA | Light-sensitive screen in the eye | The image formed on the retina is upside down.
VOLT | SI unit of potential difference | The volt is named after Alessandro Volta, who made the first battery.
FUSE | Safety device in a circuit | A fuse wire melts if too much current flows.
MAGNET | Has a north and a south pole | Magnetic field lines go from north to south outside the magnet.
NUCLEUS | Control centre of the cell | The nucleus contains chromosomes made of DNA.
GENE | Unit of heredity | Genes are segments of DNA on chromosomes.
XYLEM | Tissue that carries water in plants | Xylem has tracheids and vessels.
STOMATA | Pores on leaves for gas exchange | Guard cells open and close stomata.
CAMBIUM | Lateral meristem that thickens the stem | Cambium adds girth to trees.
AXON | Long fibre of a neuron | The axon carries impulses away from the cell body.
CORNEA | Clear front part of the eye | Most refraction of light happens at the cornea.
PUPIL | Opening in the iris | The pupil gets smaller in bright light.
IRIS | Coloured part of the eye | The iris controls the size of the pupil.
AUXIN | Plant hormone for growth towards light | Auxin collects on the shaded side of a shoot.
ZYGOTE | Fertilised egg | The zygote divides to form an embryo.
EMBRYO | Early stage of a developing organism | In plants the embryo is inside the seed.
SPORE | Tiny reproductive body of fungi | Rhizopus spreads by spores.
HYDRA | Animal that reproduces by budding | Hydra also shows regeneration.
GENUS | Group of related species | Homo is the genus of humans.
MONERA | Kingdom of bacteria | Monera have no nucleus.
FUNGI | Kingdom with chitin cell walls | Yeast and mushrooms are fungi.
PRAWN | Shellfish grown in aquaculture | Prawns are farmed in coastal ponds.
HYBRID | Offspring of two different varieties | Hybrid seeds give better yields.
MANURE | Organic fertilizer | Manure adds humus to the soil.
BIOGAS | Fuel gas made from dung | Biogas is mostly methane.
METHANE | Main gas in biogas and CNG | Methane is the simplest alkane, CH4.
ETHANOL | Alcohol in spirit | Ethanol is C2H5OH.
BENZENE | Ring compound with six carbon atoms | Benzene is C6H6.
PROPANE | Alkane with three carbon atoms | Propane is C3H8.
SOAP | Cleans by forming micelles | Soap does not lather well in hard water.
GYPSUM | Heated to make plaster of Paris | Gypsum is CaSO4·2H2O.
BRASS | Alloy of copper and zinc | Brass is used in taps and musical instruments.
BRONZE | Alloy of copper and tin | Bronze statues last for centuries.
SOLDER | Alloy of lead and tin | Solder joins electric wires.
ANODE | Positive electrode | Oxidation happens at the anode.
CATHODE | Negative electrode | Metals are deposited at the cathode.
REDOX | Reaction with oxidation and reduction | Rusting is a redox reaction.
MOLE | Amount with 6.022 × 10^23 particles | The mass of one mole is the molar mass.
CATION | Positive ion | Na+ is a cation.
ANION | Negative ion | Cl− is an anion.
NEON | Noble gas used in glowing signs | Neon has a full outer shell.
GALLIUM | Mendeleev's eka-aluminium | Gallium melts in your hand.
PERIOD | Horizontal row of the periodic table | There are seven periods.
GROUP | Vertical column of the periodic table | There are 18 groups.
NEWTON | SI unit of force | 1 N = 1 kg m/s².
PASCAL | SI unit of pressure | 1 Pa = 1 N/m².
ECHO | Reflected sound | An echo needs at least 17 m distance.
SONAR | Uses ultrasound to find sea depth | Ships use SONAR.
HERTZ | Unit of frequency | 1 Hz is one vibration per second.
AMPERE | SI unit of electric current | 1 A = 1 C/s.
CHARGE | Property measured in coulombs | Like charges repel.
MYOPIA | Short-sightedness | Corrected by a concave lens.
LENS | Transparent piece that refracts light | A convex lens converges light.
FOCUS | Point where rays meet after a lens | The focal length is the distance to the focus.
MOTOR | Turns electrical energy into motion | A motor uses Fleming's left-hand rule.
DYNAMO | Generator of electric current | Turns motion into electricity.
SOLAR | Energy from the Sun | Solar cells are made of silicon.
TIDAL | Energy from the rise and fall of the sea | Tidal energy needs dams across narrow seas.
FISSION | Splitting of a heavy nucleus | Nuclear power plants use fission of uranium.
PEPSIN | Enzyme in the stomach that digests protein | Pepsin works in acidic conditions.
ALKANE | Saturated hydrocarbon | Alkanes have only single bonds.
KIDNEY | Organ that filters blood to make urine | Each kidney has about a million nephrons.
